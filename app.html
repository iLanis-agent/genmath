The verdict

Why "4000 watts" is not 4000 watts

Generators are sold on peak watts - a surge rating that lasts seconds. What you can actually run is the running watts. Motors (fridge, furnace blower, pumps, AC) need 3x their running power to start, so the honest test is not the total - it is whether the biggest motor can kick on while everything else is already running. And runtime is not linear: at half load a typical portable sips roughly half the fuel it burns flat out, so one tank lasts much longer than the brochure's full-load number suggests.
