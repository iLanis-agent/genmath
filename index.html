GenMath

The watts on the box are a surge. Here is what you can actually run, and for how long.

The box lie

A "4000-watt" generator usually sustains 3500. The rest is a few seconds of starting surge. Sizing your storm plan off the box number is how the fridge dies at hour two.

The motor truth

Fridges, furnace blowers, pumps and ACs need 3x their running watts to start. The honest test is not your total - it is whether the biggest motor can kick on while everything else is already running.

The runtime truth

Fuel burn is not linear. At half load a portable sips roughly half what it burns flat out - so your tank lasts far longer than the full-load brochure number, if you sized the load honestly.
